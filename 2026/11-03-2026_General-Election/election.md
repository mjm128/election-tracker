## 📜 2026 State Ballot Propositions

| Measure | My Vote | Core Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Allow Public Financing of Election Campaigns** | ❌ **NO** | **Free Speech & Fiscal Restraint:** Taxpayer money should not fund political campaigns. This forces citizens to subsidize politicians and political speech they may fundamentally disagree with, expanding the state budget unnecessarily. |
| **Eliminate State Officer Recall Successor Elections** | ❌ **NO** | **Voter Disenfranchisement:** This is an establishment power grab designed to protect incumbents. By eliminating the successor election and defaulting the office to the Lieutenant Governor, it robs voters of their constitutional right to directly choose a replacement during a recall. |
| **Vote Requirements for Initiatives Requiring Supermajority Votes** *(ACA 13)* | ❌ **NO** | **Protecting Taxpayers:** A legislative trick explicitly designed to block taxpayer protection acts. It makes it mathematically harder for citizens to pass ballot initiatives that restrict the government's ability to raise taxes. |
| **Voter ID, Citizenship Verification, and Voter List Admin** | ✅ **YES** | **Election Integrity:** Common-sense governance. Requiring identification, verifying citizenship, and actively cleaning up voter rolls protects the legitimacy of the electoral process and ensures every legal vote carries its full weight. |
