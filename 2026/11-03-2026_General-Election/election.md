## 📜 2026 State Ballot Propositions
*These are the major initiatives currently qualified or pending for the November ballot. Detailed research and voting rationale will be tracked in `propositions.md` and `vote.md`.*

| Measure | Subject / Summary |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Allow Public Financing of Election Campaigns** | A measure to repeal the ban on public funding for state and local election campaigns. |
| **Eliminate State Officer Recall Successor Elections** | An amendment that would remove the second part of a recall ballot (choosing a successor) and instead allow the Lieutenant Governor to assume the office if a Governor is recalled. |
| **Vote Requirements for Initiatives Requiring Supermajority Votes** *(ACA 13)* | An amendment requiring that any ballot measure aiming to increase the voter approval threshold for future taxes/measures must itself pass by that same higher supermajority threshold. |
| **Voter Identification, Citizenship Verification, and Registered Voter List Admin** | An initiative requiring photo ID to vote, increased citizenship verification for registration, and tighter administration of state voter rolls. |
